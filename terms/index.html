2. Your documents and accuracy

Pocketdocs is a personal organiser for copies of your documents. It doesn’t verify whether a document is genuine or valid, it isn’t an official form of identification, and it doesn’t replace your original documents. Authorities, airlines and other organisations may still ask to see the original.

Pocketdocs reads documents with text recognition and, on supported iPhones, an on-device language model. These can make mistakes, so check every detail against the original before you rely on it or copy it into a form. Expiry reminders depend on the date saved in Pocketdocs and on your iPhone’s notification settings, and can be delayed or missed, so don’t rely on Pocketdocs as your only reminder.

Only add documents that belong to you, or that you’re allowed to keep, such as those of family members who have agreed.

3. Who Pocketdocs is for

Pocketdocs is for people who want to keep copies of their own and their family’s documents on their iPhone. It needs an iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Help from Apple Intelligence’s on-device model is available only on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence; on other iPhones, Pocketdocs reads documents with Apple’s Vision text recognition alone.
