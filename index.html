Why Pocketdocs

Read on your iPhone

Pocketdocs reads your documents with Apple’s on-device tools. Nothing is uploaded to be read.

Questions

Good to know

Is Pocketdocs an official ID?

No. Pocketdocs keeps copies of your documents for your own reference. It doesn’t check whether a document is genuine, and it doesn’t replace the original. Always compare the details it reads with the original.

Why is there no account?

Because we’d rather not hold your documents at all. They’re stored on your iPhone, so there’s nothing to sign in to and no copy on our side to leak.

What if I never buy Pro?

You can keep up to 3 documents with every free feature: scanning, on-device reading, search and copy, expiry reminders and the Face ID lock.

What happens when I change phones?

By default, Pocketdocs data stays on this iPhone and isn’t in your backups, so it doesn’t move to a new phone. You can turn on Include in iPhone backup in Settings to keep it in your own backup instead.

What Pocketdocs is, and what it isn’t

Pocketdocs is a personal organiser for copies of your identity and everyday documents. It lets you scan documents, read their details on your iPhone, search and copy those details, and get reminders before documents expire. Pocketdocs does not verify whether a document is genuine, is not an official form of identification, and does not replace your original documents.

Text recognition can make mistakes, so check every detail against the original before you use it. Read the Terms of Use.
