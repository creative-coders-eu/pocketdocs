Frequently asked questions

How do I add a document?

In Library, tap + (Add a document) and pick what you’re adding, or leave Detect automatically selected. Then scan it with the camera, pick a picture you already have in Photos, import a PDF from Files (papers, policies and tickets; passports and cards take photos), or type the details in. Pocketdocs reads the key details on your iPhone and shows them, so you can check and correct them, crop a page and pick dates before you save.

The camera shows corner marks where the document goes; line it up and press the shutter. Nothing is cropped automatically: to crop a page, tap Crop under it and drag the corners or edges, or tap Rotate. The first time, iOS asks for permission to use the camera. You can change it later in the iPhone Settings app under Apps → Pocketdocs.

Which documents can Pocketdocs read?

Passports, ID cards, residence permits, driving licences, insurance and payment cards, boarding passes and tickets, car papers, contracts and other everyday documents. Pocketdocs uses Apple’s Vision text recognition on your iPhone and reads the machine-readable code lines on passports and ID cards. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Apple’s on-device model also helps sort the details. It fills in the key details of each kind of document, such as the name, number and expiry date; the rest stays on the page images, and you can add any detail yourself.

Text recognition can make mistakes, especially with glare, blur or unusual layouts. Always compare the details with the original document, and correct a field if it’s wrong.

How does copying work?

Open a document or search for a detail, then tap a field to copy it and paste it into the form you’re filling in. The copy stays on this iPhone, and Pocketdocs clears it from the clipboard after 60 seconds.

To paste on your Mac or iPad instead, touch and hold the field and choose Copy to Mac or iPad. This uses Apple’s Universal Clipboard, so your devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account and near each other, with Handoff turned on. That copy also clears after 60 seconds.

How do I fill in a form with AutoFill?

AutoFill in Safari and apps is part of Pocketdocs Pro.

  1. In Pocketdocs, open Tools → AutoFill in Safari & apps and tap Turn On AutoFill. Or, in the iPhone Settings app, go to General → AutoFill & Passwords and turn on Pocketdocs.
  2. In Safari or another app, touch and hold a text field and choose AutoFill → Passwords → Pocketdocs.
  3. Pick a document and tap the detail you want. Pocketdocs inserts it into the field.

If the app lock is on, AutoFill asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode first. Pocketdocs appears in the Passwords list because that’s where iOS puts AutoFill providers, but it stores no passwords.

How does iCloud sync work?

iCloud sync is part of Pocketdocs Pro and is off until you turn it on in Tools → Sync with iCloud. Your documents are then kept in your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. We can’t read them, and neither can Apple. They use your iCloud storage.

To stop syncing, turn off Sync with iCloud. You can keep the iCloud copy or remove it; your documents stay on your iPhone either way.

How does chip reading work?

Chip reading is part of Pocketdocs Pro and needs an iPhone with NFC and a passport or ID card with a chip. Open the document and tap Read the chip. Pocketdocs uses the document’s number, date of birth and expiry date, or the card access number (CAN) on ID cards, to open the chip. Then hold the top of your iPhone flat against the document until the reading finishes.

The details from the chip replace the scanned ones, and Pocketdocs checks that the chip’s data is signed and unchanged. That check doesn’t prove that the document is genuine. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Why didn’t my expiry reminder arrive?

  • Check that the document has the right expiry date in Pocketdocs.
  • Check that notifications are on for Pocketdocs in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications → Pocketdocs.
  • Check that a Focus isn’t silencing Pocketdocs.
  • If you use Scheduled Summary, set Pocketdocs to Immediate Delivery so reminders aren’t held for the summary.

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, so they work offline, and their text names only the document type. Even so, please don’t rely on Pocketdocs as your only reminder.

How does the lock work?

When the lock is on, Pocketdocs asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode before it shows your documents, and locks again when you leave the app. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS: Pocketdocs never sees your face or fingerprint data.

Behind the lock, Pocketdocs’ data is protected by iOS Data Protection, and page images are encrypted again with a key kept in your iPhone’s Keychain.

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

You can keep up to 3 documents for free, with scanning, on-device reading, search and copy (including Copy to Mac or iPad), expiry reminders, the lock, colour themes and encrypted backup files.

Pocketdocs Pro adds unlimited documents, family members, end-to-end encrypted sync between your devices, AutoFill in Safari and apps, chip reading, watermarked copies, the trip check and renewal checklists, and widgets. It’s $19.99 a year with a 1-week free trial, $3.99 a month, or $39.99 once (US prices).

How do I restore my purchase?

Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so there’s nothing to sign in to in Pocketdocs.

  1. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy Pocketdocs Pro.
  2. In Pocketdocs, open Settings.
  3. Tap Restore purchases.

Still not working? Email us with the plan you bought and roughly when. We don’t need receipts.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Apple manages subscriptions. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Pocketdocs Pro. From there you can change your plan or tap Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from the App Store app (tap your profile picture, then Subscriptions).

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal or trial end date to avoid being charged.
  • You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
  • Deleting Pocketdocs doesn’t cancel a subscription.
  • If you buy Lifetime while subscribed, cancel the subscription yourself.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose “Request a refund” and select Pocketdocs Pro. Apple decides on refund requests; we can’t issue refunds or see your payment details.

What happens when I change iPhones?

There are three ways to bring your documents along:

  • iCloud sync (Pro): turn it on, and your documents appear on the new iPhone once it’s signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • A backup file: in Tools, export an encrypted backup with a password you choose, then import it on the new iPhone. Nobody can open the file without that password, and we can’t recover it.
  • Your iPhone backup: turn on Include in iPhone backup in Pocketdocs’ settings before you back up. It’s off by default.

Otherwise your documents stay on the old iPhone, and you’ll need to add them again.

How do I delete all my data?

In Pocketdocs’ settings, tap Delete all data to erase everything Pocketdocs has stored on your iPhone. This can’t be undone. If iCloud sync is on, your documents are also deleted from iCloud and your other devices. Your Pocketdocs Pro purchase isn’t affected. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone, but not from iCloud: to remove your iCloud copy, choose Turn off and remove iCloud copy in Tools → Sync with iCloud first.

Other copies aren’t affected: pictures you imported from Photos stay in Photos, backup files you exported stay where you saved them, and if you turned on Include in iPhone backup, older backups may still contain Pocketdocs data until they’re replaced. We don’t have a copy, so there’s nothing to delete on our side, unless you emailed us; if so, ask us to delete your emails.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s nothing to sign up for. Purchases use your Apple Account, and iCloud sync uses your own iCloud. We run no servers for Pocketdocs, so there’s no copy of your passport or ID on our side to leak.

The trade-off: we can’t recover your documents for you, so keep your originals safe and consider iCloud sync or a backup file.

Can I use Pocketdocs instead of my ID?

No. Pocketdocs keeps copies of your documents for your own reference. It doesn’t verify whether a document is genuine and isn’t an official form of identification, so carry the original whenever you need to show it. Airlines and venues may also ask for their own boarding pass or ticket. See the Terms of Use.
