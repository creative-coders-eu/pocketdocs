Frequently asked questions

How do I add a document?

Scan it with the camera, or pick a picture you already have in Photos. Pocketdocs reads the document on your iPhone and shows the details it found, so you can check and correct them before you save.

The first time you scan, iOS asks for permission to use the camera. You can change it later in the iPhone Settings app under Apps → Pocketdocs.

Which documents can Pocketdocs read?

Passports, ID cards, driving licences, insurance cards, car papers and other everyday documents. Pocketdocs uses Apple’s Vision text recognition on your iPhone and reads the machine-readable zone on passports and ID cards. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Apple’s on-device model also helps sort the details.

Text recognition can make mistakes, especially with glare, blur or unusual layouts. Always compare the details with the original document, and correct a field if it’s wrong.

How does copying work?

Search for a document or a detail, then tap a field to copy it and paste it into the form you’re filling in. The copy stays on this iPhone, so it isn’t shared with your other Apple devices, and Pocketdocs clears it from the clipboard after 60 seconds.

Why didn’t my expiry reminder arrive?

  • Check that the document has the right expiry date in Pocketdocs.
  • Check that notifications are on for Pocketdocs in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications → Pocketdocs.
  • Check that a Focus isn’t silencing Pocketdocs.
  • If you use Scheduled Summary, set Pocketdocs to Immediate Delivery so reminders aren’t held for the summary.

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, so they work offline, and their text names only the document type. Even so, please don’t rely on Pocketdocs as your only reminder.

How does the lock work?

Pocketdocs asks for Face ID, or your device passcode, before it shows your documents. Face ID is handled by iOS: Pocketdocs never sees your face data.

Behind the lock, Pocketdocs’ data is protected by iOS Data Protection, and page images are encrypted again with a key kept in your iPhone’s Keychain that never leaves this device.

What’s free, and what does Pro add?

You can keep up to 3 documents for free, with scanning, on-device reading, search and copy, expiry reminders and the lock. Pocketdocs Pro adds unlimited documents and family members. It’s $19.99 a year with a 1-week free trial, $3.99 a month, or $39.99 once (US prices).

How do I restore my purchase?

Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so there’s nothing to sign in to in Pocketdocs.

  1. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy Pocketdocs Pro.
  2. In Pocketdocs, open Settings.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

Still not working? Email us with the plan you bought and roughly when. We don’t need receipts.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Apple manages subscriptions. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Pocketdocs Pro. From there you can change your plan or tap Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from the App Store app (tap your profile picture, then Subscriptions).

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal or trial end date to avoid being charged.
  • You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
  • Deleting Pocketdocs doesn’t cancel a subscription.
  • If you buy Lifetime while subscribed, cancel the subscription yourself.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose “Request a refund” and select Pocketdocs Pro. Apple decides on refund requests; we can’t issue refunds or see your payment details.

What happens when I change iPhones?

By default, Pocketdocs keeps its data out of your iCloud and computer backups, so nothing leaves your iPhone. That also means your documents don’t move to a new iPhone, and you’ll need to scan them again there.

If you’d rather have them in your backup, turn on Include in iPhone backup in Pocketdocs’ settings. Your data then travels inside your own Apple or computer backup. We never receive it. Pocketdocs doesn’t sync between devices.

How do I delete all my data?

In Pocketdocs’ settings, tap Delete all data to erase everything Pocketdocs has stored on your iPhone. This can’t be undone. Your Pocketdocs Pro purchase isn’t affected. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

Other copies aren’t affected: pictures you imported from Photos stay in Photos, and if you turned on Include in iPhone backup, older backups may still contain Pocketdocs data until they’re replaced. We don’t have a copy, so there’s nothing to delete on our side, unless you emailed us; if so, ask us to delete your emails.

Why doesn’t Pocketdocs have an account?

Accounts mean servers, and servers mean copies of your passport and ID sitting in a database somewhere. We’d rather not hold them at all. Pocketdocs has no networking code: your documents are stored on your iPhone, Apple handles purchases, and there’s nothing to sign up for.

The trade-off: we can’t recover your data for you, and Pocketdocs doesn’t sync between devices.

Can I use Pocketdocs instead of my ID?

No. Pocketdocs keeps copies of your documents for your own reference. It doesn’t verify whether a document is genuine and isn’t an official form of identification, so carry the original whenever you need to show it. See the Terms of Use.
